Range: Between 1200px and 1920px.
Behavior: The layout is fluid (width: 100%). Elements inside should adjust their spacing (using gap: clamp(...) or percentages) to fit the screen width, but they must maintain their row alignment. Do not break rows or wrap items unless specified.
