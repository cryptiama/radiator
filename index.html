Our Laboratories

Physical-Chemical Processes

Studying the kinetics and thermodynamics of chemical reactions to understand fundamental molecular behavior.

Organic Compound Chemistry

Synthesis and analysis of new organic compounds and materials for medicinal and industrial applications.

High Molecular Compounds

Researching polymers, synthetic resins, and macromolecular structures for future materials science.

Agrarian Chemistry

Developing sustainable fertilizers and studying soil-plant chemical interactions to improve crop health.

Chemical Ecology

Monitoring environmental pollutants and ecological signaling to protect regional biodiversity.

Petrochemistry

Advanced research into petroleum products, hydrocarbon processing, and modern refining technologies.
